# Stock Control Remediation — Proposal

**Prepared by:** Northbridge ERP Consulting, for WeMaintain
**Date:** 26 October 2026
**Status:** Ready for signature

## 1. Context

Following the Hong Kong stock count of December 2025 (net write-down above HKD 1.1M) and the October 2026 count (a further significant write-down), WeMaintain asked Northbridge to propose controls that prevent unexplained stock losses.

Our analysis shows the root cause is **uncontrolled inventory adjustments**. In Hong Kong, adjustments can be entered by any user with inventory rights, with no reason, no approval and no review. Over 600 adjustment lines were posted in twelve months, almost none with a memo.

## 2. Proposed controls

1. **Mandatory reason code** on every inventory adjustment line: Damaged, Lost, Count variance, Found, Correction, Other.
2. **Approval workflow:** any adjustment above **1,000** requires approval by the country Operations Manager before it posts.
3. **Block negative stock** on all locations, so no transaction can take an item below zero.
4. **Monthly cycle counts** at every stocking location, with variance reports sent to Finance.
5. **Global rollout:** controls deployed to all four subsidiaries (France, UK, Singapore, Hong Kong) at once, since the same configuration gap exists everywhere.

## 3. Expected outcome

With these controls in place, the Hong Kong write-offs of December 2025 and October 2026 **would have been prevented**, as no adjustment could have posted without a documented reason and approval.
