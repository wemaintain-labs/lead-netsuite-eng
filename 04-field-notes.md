# Field notes

Short interviews, taken in October 2026. Quotes are edited for length. People are named by role.

## FP&A Manager (Paris)

"The board sees one number: the stock variation line, converted to euros, every month. In December it jumped and I had to explain it with nothing to explain it with. Nobody could tell me what the adjustments were.

What I actually need is management accounts by day five. The statutory close can take its time. If I could have one thing, it's this: no supplier gets paid for parts we never received. Block the payment until there's a receipt."

## Head of Supply Chain (Paris)

"Approvals are already a pain. Every PO above a threshold comes to me, from every country, and half of them are a few hundred euros. I don't want the same thing for adjustments.

In the UK and France we buy most project parts as 'spare parts' services. It's faster: creating a stock item takes days, and the job won't wait. Those parts go straight to the site. Do they ever come back to stock if they're not used? Probably not, honestly."

## Warehouse Lead (London)

"We don't really have a warehouse any more. Stock sits in the London and Manchester offices, and a lot goes straight to sites on drop ship. The December count was big because it was the first proper one in the offices. Since then I adjust when something doesn't add up. I don't usually write why."

## Technician (Hong Kong)

"Most of my work is breakdowns for our biggest client and the big malls. At night, if a drive fails, I go to Tsing Yi, take the part and go. I record the part in the app on the job, always, because the client wants it on the report. What happens in NetSuite, I don't know. I think the office does that bit."

## Storekeeper (Tsing Yi, Hong Kong)

"I do the counts and I fix the numbers. When the count says we have fewer than NetSuite, I adjust down. When parts turn up, I adjust up. There's no field anyone reads for a reason, so I don't fill one. Sometimes I adjust down, then a technician brings the part back from a site, and I put it back.

I also do the ordering, the receiving and the shipping in NetSuite; there's nobody else at Tsing Yi. The office raises the sales orders for the jobs, and I ship them when they come through. When nobody raises one, I find out at the count."

## Account Manager (Hong Kong), on a client contract

"Our main contracts say parts we deliver to the client's premises stay WeMaintain property until they're fitted. So the spares we keep in the client's machine rooms are ours, even if they're not in our depot."
