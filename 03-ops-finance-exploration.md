# Stock & Close Exploration — Autumn 2026

**Prepared by:** Ops & Finance working group
**Status:** Final. Recommendations agreed with country leads.

## Executive summary

WeMaintain lost **HKD 2.6M of stock in Hong Kong** in under a year, through repeated write-offs at the Tsing Yi depot. The other countries are broadly under control. The root cause is a lack of control over adjustments, which the partner's remediation proposal addresses. We recommend signing it and rolling it out globally before the partner leaves.

## 1. Stock health by country

**Hong Kong — critical.** Write-downs totalled HKD 2.6M between December 2025 and October 2026. Almost all were entered by one person, without explanation. High-value parts (inverters, drives) are the main losses. Physical security at Tsing Yi should be reviewed.

**UK — under control.** The December 2025 year-end count lowered stock by about £130k. This was a one-off clean-up of legacy stock from the old warehouse and has not recurred.

**France — good.** Few adjustments, mostly with explanations. Receiving discipline is strong at Stains.

**Singapore — best in class.** Only a handful of adjustments in the whole year.

## 2. Receiving discipline

We found more than 1,000 purchase order lines in the UK and France that show as received but have **no item receipt** behind them. This points to people closing POs without receiving goods properly and should be addressed through training.

## 3. Catalogue quality

Duplicates are limited: around **2% of active items** share a name with another item. A clean-up can wait until after the controls are in place.

## 4. The close

Month-end close takes 9 working days on average. The biggest time sinks are:
- reconciling supplier bills to receipts by hand (bills aren't linked to receipts);
- explaining stock variations to the board;
- revenue recognition on multi-year maintenance contracts.

### 4.1 Revenue recognition

Maintenance contracts are billed quarterly in advance and recognised monthly. Repair quotes are recognised on completion. Modernisation projects are recognised on milestones, but milestones are tracked in spreadsheets outside NetSuite, and the ARM module is only partly configured. The working group spent three sessions on this. We recommend a separate project in 2027 to configure ARM fully, migrate milestone tracking into NetSuite and align the four countries on one revenue policy. Detailed requirements are in the Finance shared drive.

## 5. Recommendations (agreed)

1. Sign the partner's remediation proposal and roll it out to all four countries in November.
2. Review physical security at Tsing Yi (CCTV, key control).
3. Move to quarterly counts in Hong Kong.
4. Receiving training for UK and France.
5. ARM project in 2027.
